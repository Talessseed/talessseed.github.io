About me

I am a 3rd year PhD student in LaBRI, Bordeaux, under the supervision of Cyril Gavoille and Marthe Bonamy. I am in the CombAlgo department and in the GO and AlgoDist teams. I defended my thesis on June 23rd, 2026. You can find my defense slides here and my manuscript here soon.

Starting from September 2026, I'll be working at Gran Sasso Science Institute, L'Aquila, Italy, with professors Alkida Balliu and Dennis Olivetti.

You can contact me on name.surname@u-bordeaux.fr.

Research interests

I am interested in discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science, and more specifically:

  • distributed computing (especially what surrounds LOCAL algorithms)
  • structural graph theory,
  • and parameterized algorithms.
